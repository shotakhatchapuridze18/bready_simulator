## Key facts about the data
- Maxima differ by economy: Pillar III when Enterprise Survey data has <6 observations (weight redistributed); Taxation when no VAT; Trade when landlocked/island/non-WTO. Frontier (best value seen) was often above an economy's real ceiling (240 of Georgia's 855 indicators).
- Pillar III indicator score = straight line between P5 and P95 of the 101 economies (lower-is-better inverted), except 3 indicators on a normal curve (Labor social contribution ratio, Taxation ETR profit, ETR employment). Some thresholds are fixed standards.
- Taxation Pillar III recovered scales: time to file/pay 12 to 200 hours (exact); tax dispute 30 to 365 days (exact); audit 1 to 8 weeks (near-exact); ETR profit normal curve mid 17.99, sd 3.50; ETR employment mid 17.99, sd 5.47; e-filing about 99% to 13% (approx); VAT refund NOT reproducible (needs firm-level survey data).
- Cost indicators in Business Entry/utilities/trade are scored as % of GNI per capita (current LCU, WDI `NY.GNP.PCAP.CN`, file uploaded; missing for Taiwan, China and South Sudan). Best year 2023 but not exact (56% of economies within 0.5 pt); plan: back out each economy's GNI from its own published score so the baseline matches exactly. Dispute Resolution costs are % of claim value, no GNI needed.

5. Negatively phrased indicators ("Absence of...") need clearer labels.
6. Flag indicators whose max is reached by fewer than 3 economies.
7. Data tab warning: "Economy Response" column mixes text and numbers (cosmetic).
